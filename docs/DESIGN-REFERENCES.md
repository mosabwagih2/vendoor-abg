## Design principles distilled

1. **Two-pane everywhere work is reviewed** (Triage, Inbox, list + peek): the queue never disappears, J/K or ↑/↓ steps through it.
2. **The next action is the loudest thing on the screen**, and it sits where the task appears (Home card, triage pane, sticky bar on mobile). Plane/Trello/Airwallex all do this; it is PRD principle 9.5.1.
3. **Waiting on is a first-class column and badge**, never inferred from colour (status icon + label + "Waiting on Vendoor" text).
4. **Properties as chips under the title on mobile, as a right column on desktop** (Plane/Jira).
5. **Filters are tokens; display is one popover; views are saved filters + display** (Linear/Sentry/Todoist).
6. **Internal vs public is always visible** (GitLab/Front tint + lock).
7. **Everything restrained:** neutral surfaces, 1 px borders, no shadows except popovers and modals (section 9.3).
